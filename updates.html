Deck Buddy is still a work in progress. This page lists what's in the release right now, what's still to come, and what changed recently.

The release: what's in it now

Early access first

Everything below is built and in the live demo. None of it has been tested at a live meet yet, and all of it can still change before the final product.

  • Coach deck and meet sheet — on deck, in the water and previous heats at once
  • Tap-to-place finishes, with splits entered by hand
  • Tags and notes on every swim
  • DQ picker with USA Swimming codes, plus printable DQ slips
  • Scoring for dual meets, championships and time trials
  • Scratches and no-shows tracked separately
  • Heat sheet, entries, relays, requests and the check-in desk
  • Program and results imports, and spreadsheet export
  • Coach dashboard for your groups and the whole team
  • Swimmer dashboard with best times, practice rate and streaks
  • Practice attendance: a 4-digit code plus a check that the swimmer is at the pool
  • Messages: announcements by group, team and group chats, direct messages
  • Schedule with practices, meets, volunteer slots and calendar subscribe
  • Live meet-day updates for coaches, swimmers and parents
  • Team and swimmer profiles
  • Race-day checklists
  • Team manager: coaches, groups and join codes
  • Safe Sport flags and reporting
  • Meet director and officials tools
  • Season and league stats

Still to come

Planned
  • Timing-system connection — touchpad places, times and automatic splits
  • Accounts that sync across devices — today the demo saves on the device you use
  • A phone app for swimmers and parents

Live demo and website refresh

New

November 24, 2026

  • The real meet sheet now runs on the demo page, with a demo video on the homepage.
  • New Early access page: sign up to test, free while testing.
  • Calls with Cooper are now 20–40 minutes.
  • The site now follows your device's light or dark setting.

Deck and results updates

September 15, 2026

  • On deck, In the water and Previous are all visible at once on the coach deck — no more collapsing between them mid-heat.
  • Scratches and no-shows are tracked separately: a planned scratch shows "X", an unscratched no-show shows "NS" — both excluded from placing and scoring.
  • Results imports merge finals straight onto a loaded meet program by event and name, or load as a standalone meet from just the results sheet.
  • Season & League stats: high points and improvement% by age group, filterable by team, gender and age group.
  • Time trials added as their own meet type — no points or places, excluded from improvement%.

Marketing site launched

September 9, 2026

  • deckbuddy.app went live with Features, Demo and Book-a-call pages.
  • Positioning made explicit site-wide: this is a prototype, not yet tested at a live meet, and nothing costs anything until full integration.

Deck Buddy is still a work in progress. This page tracks what's shipped in the current build, and what's coming next — updated every time a new dev release goes out.

Current build 0.0.M

Latest

September 15, 2026

  • On deck, In the water and Previous are all visible at once on the coach deck — no more collapsing between them mid-heat.
  • Scratches and no-shows are tracked separately: a planned scratch shows "X", an unscratched no-show shows "NS" — both excluded from placing and scoring.
  • Results imports merge finals straight onto a loaded meet program by event and name, or load as a standalone meet from just the results sheet.
  • Season & League stats: high points and improvement% by age group, filterable by team, gender and age group.
  • Time trials added as their own meet type — no points or places, excluded from improvement%.

Marketing site launched

September 9, 2026

  • deckbuddy.app went live with Features, Demo and Book-a-call pages.
  • Positioning made explicit site-wide: this is a prototype, not yet tested at a live meet, and nothing costs anything until full integration.

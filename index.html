Try the meet sheet yourself

Write-once

A meet sheet gets one clean pass, at the table, before the meet starts. Every mark after that — a DQ, a drop, a note about a rough start — gets squeezed into a margin or just remembered.

Unsearchable

Ask how your 9–10 girls did on turns this season and paper has no answer. The information exists. It's on forty sheets in a binder, if it's anywhere at all.

Gone by August

End of season, the binder goes in a box. Next year's coach starts from nothing — no history, no trend, no idea what the team actually needed to work on.

Anatomy of the deck

1

On deck

The next heat. Lane 1 to the highest lane, in an auto-sliding strip. Toggle between the whole heat and just your swimmers. Tap a card for that swimmer.

2

In the water

The heat racing now. Collapsed until you hit Start — starting expands it and arms tap-to-place. Heat nav on either side, a red End button, and it auto-advances once every lane has a time.

3

Previous

The heat that just finished. Shows heat place — full-event places wait until the whole event is done. Nothing about a finished heat is locked; it's still clickable.

Timing modes

Runs without a timing system today. With one, later.

No timing system Working now

Hit Start, then tap swimmers' names as they finish. Each tap assigns the next heat place. Heat place only, no times. No keyboard, no stopwatch, no typing — you can do it while watching the water.

  1. 1 Hit Start — the heat arms for tap-to-place.
  2. 2 Tap each swimmer's name as they touch the wall.
  3. 3 The heat closes itself once every lane has a place.

Connected to a timing system Planned

Deck Buddy can't talk to a timing system yet — it's a feature that comes later. When it lands, the timing system becomes the source of truth: places and times come from it and touchpad splits arrive on their own, leaving you with tagging and notes.

One app for the whole season

Put away the clipboard, the group text and three other apps.

Most teams juggle a paper meet sheet, a chat app, a results site and a meet app. Deck Buddy does each of those jobs, and they all share one roster.

Instead of paper meet sheets

Ready Room meet sheet

On deck, in the water and previous heats at once. Tap to place, tag a swim, leave a note, log a DQ mid-race.

Instead of team chat apps

Messages & schedule

Announcements by group, team and group chats, DMs, practices, meets and volunteer slots on one calendar.

Instead of results sites

Roster & profiles

A page for the team and every swimmer, with best times plus the notes, tags and attendance behind them.

Instead of meet apps

Entries & results

Enter meets, build relays, run the check-in desk, and keep results with your notes attached.

Instead of lost slips

One-tap DQs

Log the DQ on your phone and send it to the swimmer and their parent before they're back at the tent.

Built in, not bolted on

Safe Sport tools

Messages are screened and flagged for a designated reporter, who can still see everything. Supports your policy; doesn't replace it.

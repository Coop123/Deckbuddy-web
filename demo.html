Try it yourself

Four things to try first.

1

Start the heat

Open In the water and hit Start. The heat arms for tap-to-place and the lane boxes go live.

2

Tap to place

Tap each swimmer's name as they finish. The order you tap sets the heat place — no typing.

3

Tag the swim

Open a swimmer's popover and drop a tag — Turns, Streamline, whatever needs work. It rolls into the tag engine.

4

Read the sheet

Switch to the meet sheet. The running heat is highlighted, so it stays in sync with the deck.

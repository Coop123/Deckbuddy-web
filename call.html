Questions

      
Before you book

      

        

          Is it ready to use?
          

            
It's built and used on a real summer-league deck, but it's a
              pre-release build — not yet a paid product. Current build is 0.0.D. The call is about
              seeing it work, not signing anything.

          

        

        

          Does it work without a timing system?
          

            
Yes. Hit Start, then tap swimmers' names as they finish — each tap
              assigns the next heat place. Heat place only, no times, no keyboard, no stopwatch.

          

        

        

          What about our timing system's results?
          

            
When you're connected to a timing system, it's the source of truth —
              places and times come from it, and touchpad splits arrive on their own. Your job on
              deck is tagging and notes.

          

        

        

          Does it cost anything?
          

            
Not decided yet. This isn't a paid product right now — the call is a
              conversation about your program, not a sales pitch.

          

        

        

          Can our swimmers see it?
          

            
There's a swimmer-facing side in progress — pinning a swimmer's next
              event, heat, and lane, plus their results so far. It's part of what's next, not this
              build.
